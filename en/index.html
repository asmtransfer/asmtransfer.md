Before the trip

QUESTIONS ABOUT RESERVATIONS

Can I book a round trip?

For a round trip, the first hour of waiting is free, each subsequent hour is 60 rubles. PMR (≈ $3.75). The return journey and total cost will be agreed upon before confirming the order.

What do I need to tell you to order?

Write the date, time, pick-up and destination addresses, number of passengers and luggage. For an airport, add the flight number; for traveling with a child, add their age.

How is the price calculated?

The price is agreed upon in advance per vehicle. Inform about stops, waits and return journeys before confirming your order.

Need to come to the office?

No, we will coordinate the trip remotely by phone or via messenger. The driver delivers the car to a pre-agreed address.
