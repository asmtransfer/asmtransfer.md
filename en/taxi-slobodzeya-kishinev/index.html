QUESTIONS ABOUT THE TRIP

How to order a taxi Slobodzeya - Chisinau?

Inform the date, time, delivery address in the city of Slobodzeya, destination, number of passengers and luggage. We will confirm the cost and delivery time before the trip.

How much does the trip cost?

Calculation of route and price before departure. The result depends on the exact addresses, stops and wait times. The price is agreed upon per vehicle, not per passenger.

Can I order Chisinau - Slobodzeya?

Yes, the return route Chisinau - Slobodzeya is available by reservation. Agree on a return date and time, if already known.

How much time should I allow for the journey?

The time depends on the points of departure and destination, traffic conditions and possible waits at checkpoints. When ordering, please let us know what time you need to arrive.

Can I travel with a child and suitcases?

Yes. Please inform the child's age and the amount of luggage in advance to coordinate a child seat and a suitable car.
