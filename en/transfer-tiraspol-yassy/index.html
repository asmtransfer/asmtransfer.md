TIRASPOL - YASSI NON-TRANSFER

The trip is suitable for departure from Iasi airport, a trip to the city itself, a hotel, a clinic, a train station or to another address. Unlike the route to Chisinau, this is an international trip, so when planning it is important to take into account the possible wait at the border.

When booking, please provide the date, desired time of arrival, pickup address, destination, number of passengers and luggage. If you are traveling to a flight, add the flight number and departure time. We will coordinate the delivery time and cost of the car in advance.

For a return trip from Iasi to Tiraspol, you can order a meeting in the city or at the airport. After arrival, we stay in touch via messenger, agree on a meeting point and take you to the desired address in Tiraspol.
