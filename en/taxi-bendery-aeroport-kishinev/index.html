Useful information

WHAT TO EXPECT ON YOUR AIRPORT TRANSFER

The trip starts at your home, office or hotel in Bendery and ends at the Chișinău Airport terminal. When booking, please provide the date, flight number, departure time, pickup address, number of passengers and luggage. If you are traveling with a child, please indicate their age - we will prepare a child seat in advance upon request.

For the return route Chișinău Airport - Bendery, provide the flight number and arrival time. After the plane lands, we stay in touch; After receiving your luggage, we will agree on a meeting place and take you directly to the desired address in Bendery without transfers.

If the flight time has changed, please inform the new time as soon as possible - we will check the possibility of adjusting your pickup time. We confirm the cost of the trip before departure so that the conditions are clear in advance.

If you need an address in Chisinau itself, and not the airport, see the page Taxi Bendery - Chisinau. There is a separate route for trips from Tiraspol Tiraspol - Chișinău Airport.

Frequently asked questions

TAXI BENDERY - CHIȘINĂU AIRPORT: QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

How much does the trip cost?

Preliminary cost - 440 PMR rubles (≈ $27.50) per car. Before booking, we confirm the current price.

How long does the journey take?

Typically around 50-75 minutes. Allow extra time for the drive, security and check-in.

Can I book a pickup at Chișinău Airport?

Yes. We carry out return trips and agree in advance on the meeting place after baggage collection.

Do you operate at night?

Yes, we accept pre-bookings 24 hours a day, including early and night flights.

Is there a child seat?

Yes. Please advise the child's age when booking so that a suitable seat can be prepared.

What should I tell you about my luggage?

Write down the number of suitcases and large luggage - we will check the comfortable placement in advance.
