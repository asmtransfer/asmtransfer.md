Useful information

HOW IS THE TRANSFER BENDERY - CHISINAU AIRPORT?

The trip starts at your home, office or hotel in Bendery and ends at the Chisinau airport terminal. When booking, please provide the date, flight number, departure time, delivery address, number of passengers and luggage. If you are traveling with a child, please indicate their age - we will prepare a child seat in advance upon request.

For the return route Chisinau Airport - Bendery, provide the flight number and arrival time. After the plane lands, we stay in touch; After receiving your luggage, we will agree on a meeting place and take you directly to the desired address in Bendery without transfers.

If the flight time has changed, please inform the new time as soon as possible - we will check the possibility of adjusting the delivery. We confirm the cost of the trip before departure so that the conditions are clear in advance.

If you need an address in Chisinau itself, and not the airport, see the page Taxi Bendery - Chisinau. There is a separate route for trips from Tiraspol Tiraspol - Chisinau airport.
