INTERCITY TAXI IN MOLDOVA NON-TRANSFER

ASM Transfer carries out individual trips between the cities of Moldova upon prior reservation. This format is suitable for trips to the airport, clinic, train station, hotel, business meeting, relatives or tourist sites. Only you and your fellow travelers are traveling in the car - without extraneous passengers.

To calculate, please provide the starting and ending points, date, desired time, number of passengers and luggage. If you are traveling with a child, indicate the age in advance. Before the trip, we confirm the price for the car and the delivery time.
