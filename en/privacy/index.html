STATISTICS AND PRIVACY

Updated: October 26, 2026.

Your choice

The site uses Google Analytics only after selecting "Allow statistics". The analytics tag is not loaded until you agree. You can select “No statistics”: the site, contacts and booking will continue to work.

What counts

After consent, Google Analytics receives data about the page visited, the referral source and the click on the contact method (for example, WhatsApp or phone). The service also processes technical data about the browser and device and uses cookies for visit statistics. Clicking on a contact does not mean a confirmed order.

We do not send a phone number, message content, passenger name, or travel address to contact events. Arbitrary parameters and fragments of the page address are excluded; Only short advertising source tags are allowed. Ad personalization and Google Signals are disabled.

How to change your mind

The "Statistics Settings" button at the bottom of any page opens the selection again. If you refuse, further sending of events is disabled, and Google Analytics cookies available to the site are deleted. Your choices are saved in your browser for up to 180 days. This does not automatically delete data already processed by Google.

Contact and booking

When you click on the messenger, you go to the selected service. The message is sent only by your action; processing of data in the messenger is governed by its terms and conditions. The site does not automatically send the passenger message.

Questions about site statistics can be directed to ASM Transfer by phone +373 779 50 129.
