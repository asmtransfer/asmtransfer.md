Before departure

TRANSFER FROM TIRASPOL TO THE TERMINAL

To order a trip to Chișinău Airport, please provide the date, flight number, departure time, pickup address in Tiraspol, number of passengers and luggage. After this, we will coordinate the departure time and confirm the cost in advance.

This format is especially convenient for early and night flights, trips with children and a large number of suitcases: there is no need to look for transport with transfers or adjust to the minibus schedule. The car arrives at your address and takes you straight to the airport.

Wi-Fi and water are available on the road. If you require a child seat, please indicate the child's age when booking.

After arrival

CHIȘINĂU AIRPORT - TIRASPOL

The return transfer can be booked in advance along with the trip to the airport or separately. When ordering, please provide the flight number and passenger contact information. After the plane lands, we stay in touch, and after receiving the luggage, we will agree on a meeting place.

If the flight time has changed, simply advise the new time. We will check if it is possible to adjust your pickup time. After the meeting, we will help with luggage and take you directly to your destination in Tiraspol.
