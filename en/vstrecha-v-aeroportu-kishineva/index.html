AIRPORT PICKUP
CHISINAU

How to book a car, find a driver after arrival and prepare a trip with children and luggage. We will agree on the terms of a specific order with you in advance.

MeetingWith childrenBaggage

What to write when booking

  • Date, flight number and time of arrival or departure.
  • Origin and destination address, including additional stops.
  • Number of passengers and age of children who need a seat.
  • Number of suitcases, strollers and large luggage.
  • Passenger's phone number and convenient messenger, especially if you are booking for a relative.
  • Is a return trip necessary and on what day.

Before confirming the order, we will agree on the price for the car, delivery, method of meeting and waiting conditions. Please keep the driver in contact until departure.

After arrival

  1. The plane has landed. Text the driver that you are there. If the connection comes later, please let me know when you can.
  2. Receiving your luggage. Go through the necessary procedures and pick up your suitcases. If your luggage is delayed, notify the driver.
  3. Ready to go. Contact again and confirm the meeting point. By agreement, the driver will meet you with a sign or drive up to the agreed exit.
  4. Let's go home. The driver will help you place your luggage and take you to the address on your application.

If the flight is changed or delayed, transmit new information as soon as possible. We will agree with you on the wait and possible change in delivery. If the passenger is unable to use mobile Internet, we will arrange a call or another method of meeting in advance.

If you are going to the airport

Provide your departure time and the time the airline recommends you arrive for check-in. At the delivery address, we will coordinate departure with reserve for the road and possible waiting at checkpoints.

Write about stops and pick-up for other passengers when booking - this affects the route and pickup time.

Traveling with a child

Please indicate the age of each child and whether a child seat is required. If you use your own chair, please warn us about it. We will confirm the availability of a suitable seat before the trip.

Include the stroller in your luggage list. If it is large or does not fold completely, please send the folded dimensions or a photo to check placement with the suitcases.

Suitcases and large luggage

Write the number of suitcases and hand luggage. For large suitcases, bicycles, boxes or other non-standard cargo, please indicate the dimensions or send a photo.

We select the car according to the number of passengers and all luggage together. Before confirming the order, we will check whether the Toyota Prius+ is suitable or whether another car option is needed.

Meeting after the bus or bus

If you are arriving in Chisinau by bus or bus, including from Bulgaria, please provide the name of the carrier, expected time of arrival and drop-off point. For example, if the carrier drops you off in the McDonald's parking lot, send the exact location on the map.

We will coordinate the parking location and meeting time for your flight. Then we will take you to Parcani, Bendery, Tiraspol or another agreed address. If your arrival is delayed, please notify the driver.
