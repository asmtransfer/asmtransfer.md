Before travelling to Tiraspol

Travel date and time, pickup and destination addresses, passenger count and luggage. For airport pickup, include your flight number. For a child seat, tell us the child’s age.

What to send when booking

Travel date and time, pickup and destination addresses, passenger count and luggage. For airport pickup, include your flight number. For a child seat, tell us the child’s age.

Travel documents and entry conditions

Check current requirements for your nationality and documents using official sources before travelling. Your transfer provider arranges transport but does not decide admission at checkpoints. Do not enter passport details or upload passport photos in the booking form.

Timing and contact

Allow extra time for traffic and possible checkpoint delays. Save the driver’s number and destination address. Agree a contact method in case mobile data is unavailable.

Price and payment

Prices are per vehicle in PMR rubles. US dollar amounts are approximate at 16 PMR rubles per $1. Agree the payment currency, method, stops and total price before confirming.

Airport pickup

Send your flight number and contact the driver after collecting your luggage. Agree the meeting point in advance and wait for confirmation before looking for the car.

Official sources to check before travel

  • Moldova Border Police
  • General Inspectorate for Migration
  • UK travel advice — Moldova
