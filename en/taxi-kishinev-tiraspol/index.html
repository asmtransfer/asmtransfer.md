QUESTIONS ABOUT THE TRIP

How do I book a taxi from Chisinau to Tiraspol?

Send your travel date and time, pickup address in Chisinau, destination in Tiraspol, number of passengers and luggage details. We confirm the price and pickup time before your trip.

How much does the trip cost?

From 600 PMR rubles (≈ $37.50) per car. The final price depends on your exact addresses, additional stops and waiting time. The price is agreed upon per vehicle, not per passenger.

Can I book a taxi from Tiraspol to Chisinau?

Yes. You can book a private taxi from Tiraspol to Chisinau as a separate trip or arrange a return journey in advance.

How much time should I allow for the journey?

The time depends on the points of departure and destination, traffic conditions and possible waits at checkpoints. When ordering, please let us know what time you need to arrive.

Can I travel with a child and suitcases?

Yes. Please inform the child's age and the amount of luggage in advance to coordinate a child seat and a suitable car.
