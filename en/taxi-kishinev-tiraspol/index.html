QUESTIONS ABOUT THE TRIP

How to order a taxi Chisinau - Tiraspol?

Inform the date, time, delivery address in the city of Chisinau, destination, number of passengers and luggage. We will confirm the cost and delivery time before the trip.

How much does the trip cost?

Preliminary from 600 rub. PMR (≈ $37.50) per car. The result depends on the exact addresses, stops and wait times. The price is agreed upon per vehicle, not per passenger.

Can I order Tiraspol - Chisinau?

Yes, the return direction Tiraspol - Chisinau is available by reservation. Agree on a return date and time, if already known.

How much time should I allow for the journey?

The time depends on the points of departure and destination, traffic conditions and possible waits at checkpoints. When ordering, please let us know what time you need to arrive.

Can I travel with a child and suitcases?

Yes. Please inform the child's age and the amount of luggage in advance to coordinate a child seat and a suitable car.
