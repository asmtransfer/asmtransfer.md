Trip to Romania

BENDERY - IASSY AND AIRPORT DIRECTLY

The route is suitable for departure from Iasi airport, travel to the city center, hotel, clinic, train station or to another address. The car is delivered to your address in Bendery, and the trip is non-stop.

When booking, please indicate the date, desired time of arrival, pick-up address, destination, number of passengers and luggage capacity. If you are traveling to a flight, add the flight number and departure time. Since the trip is international, when choosing a departure time, we take into account the possible wait at the border.

Return transfer Iasi - Bendery is also available. When meeting after arrival, we stay in touch, agree on a meeting point and deliver directly to the desired address.
